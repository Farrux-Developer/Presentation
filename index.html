02 // Vaadhoo, Maldives

Sea of
Stars

At night, the beaches of Vaadhoo Island glow neon-blue. As waves touch the shore, the water literally erupts in light due to bioluminescent phytoplankton. Imagine swimming in a liquid night sky.

03 // Lake Hillier, Australia

Bubblegum
Lake

Lake Hillier looks as if someone poured millions of gallons of strawberry milkshake right onto an island. The craziest part? The water stays bright pink even if you scoop it into a glass bottle!
